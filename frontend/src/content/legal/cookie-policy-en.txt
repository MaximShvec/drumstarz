2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. Browser local storage and other technologies may serve similar purposes. They can support website functionality, remember preferences or, with your consent, help analyse visits and measure advertising.

3. Categories and consent

Strictly necessary cookies are used only to the extent required for the website to operate or to provide a function you expressly request. These cookies do not require consent.

Preference, analytics and marketing technologies that are not strictly necessary require your consent before activation. You may accept or reject them by category. Refusing them does not prevent you from applying for a free lesson.

Continuing to browse or closing a notice does not constitute consent.

You can use this tool at any time to change your choice or withdraw consent as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

You can also delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may affect certain functions.
